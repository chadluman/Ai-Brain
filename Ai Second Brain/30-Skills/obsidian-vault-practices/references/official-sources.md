# Official Obsidian Sources

Verified 2026-10-21. Product behavior was checked against official Obsidian material and the current public desktop release, [Obsidian 1.14.4](https://obsidian.md/changelog/2026-10-05-desktop-v1.14.4/), released 2026-10-05. The Help site is living documentation, so recheck affected pages for version-sensitive advice.

## Source Roles

- [Obsidian Help](https://obsidian.md/help/) is the primary operating documentation for the application and official services.
- [Obsidian changelog](https://obsidian.md/changelog/) and the canonical [release repository](https://github.com/obsidianmd/obsidian-releases) are the primary sources for current releases and changes.
- [Developer documentation](https://docs.obsidian.md/) is authoritative for plugin, theme, API, and directory-policy behavior.
- [License overview](https://obsidian.md/license), [Privacy Policy](https://obsidian.md/privacy), and [Security](https://obsidian.md/security) are the primary sources for legal, privacy, and audit claims.
- Pricing, plan limits, and service behavior are volatile; verify them at the point of a recommendation or purchase rather than relying on stored figures.

## Evidence Boundaries

- Official marketing and Help material are authoritative for documented product behavior, but not proof that a particular local setup works.
- The Obsidian application is proprietary; public documentation, APIs, selected extensions, formats, and audit reports do not make the full application open source.
- Community directory inclusion and scorecards are signals, not a substitute for exact-version review.
- Security audits establish what was examined at a point in time, not a guarantee against future vulnerabilities or unsafe plugins.
- This skill summarizes and links to official material; it does not redistribute the external documentation.
