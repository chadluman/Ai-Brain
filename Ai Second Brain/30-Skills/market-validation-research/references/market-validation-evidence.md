# Market Validation Evidence

Verified: 2026-10-21

## Primary Sources

- [GOV.UK: Understand users and their needs](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/service-standard/point-1-understand-user-needs) says to understand the full user context, focus on the problem rather than a presumed solution, combine research with available data, and test assumptions early.
- [GOV.UK: Learning about users and their needs](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/start-by-learning-user-needs) distinguishes user outcomes from features and calls for continuing research through discovery, alpha, beta, and live operation.
- [GOV.UK: How the discovery phase works](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/agile-delivery/how-the-discovery-phase-works) frames discovery as learning before committing to build and requires a clear goal, constraints, problem definition, and finish condition.
- [U.S. Small Business Administration: Plan your business](https://www.sba.gov/counseling/plan-your-business/) covers demand, market size, location, saturation, pricing, direct and secondary research, competitors, entry barriers, and public statistical sources.
- [NSF I-Corps](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/initiatives/i-corps) documents an experiential approach intended to reduce the risk of translating an invention to market; it is useful evidence for testing commercialization assumptions, not proof that one curriculum fits every product.

## Decision Boundaries

- These sources support problem-first and evidence-first discovery. They do not define a universal sample size, research duration, conversion threshold, or market-sizing formula.
- Market size is conditional on the segment definition, buying process, reach, adoption, price, capacity, competition, regulation, and time horizon. Record every input and show sensitivity.
- Competitor marketing, marketplace listings, app-store rankings, social engagement, search interest, and funding announcements are leads to investigate, not independent proof of demand or product quality.
- Primary research can reveal language, context, behavior, and unmet needs but remains vulnerable to recruitment and response bias. Pair what people say with observed behavior or operational evidence where proportionate.
- A landing page, preorder, waitlist, or paid acquisition test changes external state and may create consumer, privacy, advertising, payment, and brand obligations. Run it only with explicit authorization and reviewed disclosures.
