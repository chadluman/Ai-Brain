# Templates Map

Copy a template into the appropriate project, review, catalog, or playbook folder before filling it in. Preserve the template itself for reuse.

| Template | Use it when | Result |
|---|---|---|
| [Project Intake](Project%20Intake.md) | Starting a product, major feature, or material redesign | Decision-ready requirements, risks, constraints, and open questions |
| [Interface Design Brief](Interface%20Design%20Brief.md) | Defining or materially redesigning a website or application interface | Traceable experience contract, design thesis, state inventory, visual architecture, provenance, and validation plan |
| [Security Review](Security%20Review.md) | Considering an external skill, script, dependency, extension, template, service, or executable asset | Version-specific evidence and an approval, conditional approval, quarantine, rejection, or retirement decision |
| [Technology ADR](Technology%20ADR.md) | Making a material architecture or provider choice | Durable decision, rationale, evidence, consequences, and exit path |
| [Service Integration Playbook](Service%20Integration%20Playbook.md) | Connecting or maintaining a provider, API, SDK, webhook, or hosted service | Reproducible setup, verification, operations, and removal guidance |

Templates are starting structures. Remove irrelevant sections from copied notes and add project-specific evidence when it improves the decision or operation.
