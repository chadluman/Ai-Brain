## How to Use This Map

- Use a shortlist to narrow a decision to two or three shapes, then re-run the matching skill before writing an ADR.
- No catalog note approves a provider or a dependency. Recheck pricing, quotas, regions, and security features from the vendor's own pages before an ADR is accepted.
- When a new shortlist is added, add its row to the Shortlists table above; when a shortlist is retired, remove its row here rather than leaving a dead link.
